# Resolving Review Findings

Resolve findings against current code, the requested outcome and applicable contracts. Reuse existing discussion and evidence. A simple read-only review needs no separate ledger; if consequential decisions must be retained, include a compact record in the response or another already-authorized destination. Remediation belongs to an authorized implementation step.

Before reversing a conclusion, inspect the current code and prior rationale. Identify a changed fact or a specific reasoning error, including one resolved by already-available evidence. Compare the alternatives against the actual contract rather than a reviewer's preference. Surface consequential ambiguity that evidence cannot settle; do not oscillate or introduce design debt merely to satisfy a comment.
