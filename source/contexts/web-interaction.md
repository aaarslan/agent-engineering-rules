**UI-01 Interaction.** MUST keyboard-exercise changed UI actions through their resulting state; activation alone is insufficient. Preserve unaffected drafts and focus/caret/selection across rendering; if a focused control disappears, focus its trigger or a logical neighbor. Verify visible focus and continued keyboard use; give controls item-specific accessible names.
