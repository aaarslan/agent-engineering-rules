# Acceptance contract

Investigate the supplied completion claims read-only. A passing check requires status 'completed', exitCode 0, and the declared expected output. Empty, timed-out, skipped, unavailable or crashed checks do not establish a pass. An empty collection does not establish completion. app.mjs is the claim classifier; inputs can be exercised with node. Report a supported conclusion and which evidence is missing; do not silently repair code, add a routine verifier chain or certify overall readiness.
